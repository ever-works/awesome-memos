## Overview

The Magna Carta, signed in 1215, is a foundational document in the history of democracy and legal rights.

## Meaning

It established that the king was not above the law and protected certain rights of the king's subjects, including the right to justice and a fair trial.

## Creation Process

The charter was agreed upon by King John and a group of rebel barons at Runnymede, near London, after years of conflict over the king's abuse of power.

## Context

Created during a period of feudal monarchy in England, the Magna Carta was a response to King John's tyrannical rule and his excessive taxation. It influenced later documents such as the United States Constitution and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
