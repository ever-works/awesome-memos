## Overview

Charles Colson's Memorandum is a directive sent by Charles Colson, Special Counsel to President Richard Nixon, to White House staff during the 1972 reelection campaign.

## Content

The memo set rules and limited time off for staff during the 24-hour, 7-day-a-week campaign, promising to make amends for bruised feelings after the election.

## Historical Context

Charles Colson served as special counsel to President Nixon from 1969 to 1973. The memorandum reflects the intense work environment during the Nixon reelection campaign.

## Significance

The document provides insight into the internal operations and management style of the Nixon White House during a pivotal election year.
