# Completed Staff Work from the Pentagon in WW2 shared by Thomas Watson

Pentagon memo during WW2 on completing staff work without annoying the boss, later shared by IBM's Thomas Watson. Created as military guidance on task completion and communication, it emphasizes self-sufficiency. Shared internally at IBM for management lessons.
