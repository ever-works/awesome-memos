# Good Product Manager Bad Product Manager

Ben Horowitz of a16z details qualities of good, bad, and great product managers in this early 2000s memo. Created to guide hiring and training at Netscape, it defines core responsibilities and pitfalls. Essential reading for understanding PM roles in tech.
