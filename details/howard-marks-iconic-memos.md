# Howard Marks’ Iconic Memos

## Overview

The Howard Marks’ Iconic Memos collection comprises a complete bound set of memos authored by Howard Marks, co-chairman and co-founder of Oaktree Capital Management. The set was inscribed by Mr. Marks and donated to the Museum of American Finance in October 2025, coinciding with the 35th anniversary of his first memo mailed in 1990.

## Creation Process

Marks began writing memos in 1990 to share candid commentary on the market environment with clients. Over three decades, he authored approximately 160 public memos, each distributed to Oaktree clients and later made publicly available. The memos are known for making complex financial ideas clear and accessible.

## Context and Significance

The memos are followed by a global audience of investors, business leaders, and academics. They are regarded as some of the most influential writings in modern finance, with praise from figures such as Warren Buffett. In 2024, Marks received the Museum of American Finance’s Lifetime Achievement Award for his contributions to the financial community.

## Related Publications

- A full compendium of the memos is available as a PDF on Oaktree’s website.
- A special curated edition, *The Best of…*, features memos deemed most impactful by Marks, each with a short introduction contextualizing their enduring relevance.

## Preservation

The bound set is now part of the Museum of American Finance’s permanent collection, ensuring preservation and public access to these seminal works in financial history.
