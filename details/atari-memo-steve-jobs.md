# Atari Memo - Steve Jobs

## Overview
This memo is a digitized image of a handwritten note authored by Steve Jobs while working at Atari, Inc. The document bears the "All One Farm" stamp, referencing Jobs' later spiritual retreat and apple orchard property.

## Description
The memo consists of handwritten text on what appears to be notebook paper. The content of the memo is not transcribed in the file description, but its historical significance lies in its origin from Jobs' early career period at Atari. ## Historical Context
Steve Jobs worked at Atari in the mid-1970s before co-founding Apple. This memo represents an artifact from that period of his career. The "All One Farm" reference connects to Jobs' later life, as he owned the All One Farm property in Oregon during the 1980s.
