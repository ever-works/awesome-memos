## Overview

Andrew Bosworth, VP of Facebook, authored this internal memo on December 30, 2019, addressing the company's approach to political advertising ahead of the 2020 U.S. elections.

## Key Points

- Bosworth argues that Facebook should not alter its advertising policies or behavior to influence election outcomes, specifically to prevent a Trump re-election.
- He cites philosopher John Rawls' concept of the "veil of ignorance," suggesting that fair decisions require ignoring personal stakes.
- The memo reflects on the fallout from the 2016 election and the ongoing tension between platform neutrality and societal impact.

## Context

The memo surfaced publicly via a New York Times article in January 2020, providing insight into Facebook's internal strategy debates during a period of intense scrutiny over election interference and misinformation.

## Significance

It illustrates how tech executives grapple with ethical dilemmas around platform influence on democratic processes, using philosophical frameworks to justify policy choices.
