# 11 Laws of Showrunning

Javier Grillo-Marxuach wrote two internal memos outlining 11 laws for effective showrunning in television production, including a blunt 'mean' version. These memos provide guidance on managing creative teams, decision-making, and production challenges. They reveal how TV executives communicate and think in high-stakes environments.
