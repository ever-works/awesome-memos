## Overview

Andrew Bosworth, VP of Facebook, wrote this internal memo in June 2016 titled "The Ugly," addressing ethical concerns about the platform's societal impact while defending aggressive growth strategies.

## Key Points

- Bosworth asserts that Facebook's core purpose is to connect people, and that this goal justifies tolerating harmful side effects (e.g., terrorist coordination).
- He argues that growth-oriented features, such as address book importing, are essential to achieving the mission, even if they raise privacy or safety concerns.
- The memo reflects a philosophy of prioritizing connection over potential misuse.

## Context

The memo became public via a BuzzFeed News article in 2016, sparking debate about Facebook's responsibility for content and the trade-offs between openness and safety.

## Significance

It captures an early articulation of Facebook's growth mindset and the ethical dilemmas that would later intensify as the platform's influence on global events grew.
