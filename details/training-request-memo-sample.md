## Overview

Training request memos communicate an employee's need for professional development training to management. They outline the purpose, expected outcomes, and justification for the training.

## Key Elements

- Explains why the employee requires specific training
- Concise and to the point for clear understanding
- Includes necessary details like training details, dates, and costs if applicable

## Format Details

- File Format: Docs
- Size: 13.3 KB

## Usage

Submit to supervisors or HR to request approval for training programs, ensuring alignment with company goals.
