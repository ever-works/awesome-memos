# Brevity by Winston Churchill

Prime Minister Winston Churchill's memo three months into WW2 tenure, urging brevity in communications by telling people to 'get to the f’n point'. Written to combat verbose wartime bureaucracy, it emphasizes concise thinking and expression. This historical document illustrates leadership communication during crisis.
