# Fix it or changes

Neal Patterson, CEO of Cerner Corporation, sent a fiery email to managers urging them to motivate staff harder or face consequences. Written to boost performance amid perceived laziness, it backfired publicly. This memo illustrates aggressive leadership tactics and their risks in corporate communication.
