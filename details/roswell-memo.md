## Overview

The Roswell Memo is a photograph of a telegram held by General Roger M. Ramey during a press conference about the 1947 Roswell incident. The memo has been the subject of intense scrutiny and debate regarding UFO conspiracy theories and weather balloon explanations.

## Meaning

The memo is alleged by some to contain phrases such as "victims of the wreck" suggesting extraterrestrial involvement, while others claim it reads weather balloon related text. It symbolizes the controversy and government secrecy surrounding the Roswell UFO incident.

## Creation Process

The photo was taken by James Bond Johnson, a photographer from the Fort Worth Star Telegram, during Ramey's press conference on July 8, 1947, in Fort Worth, Texas. Johnson captured the image of Ramey displaying debris from the alleged crashed object, which the military claimed was a weather balloon.

## Context

In 1947, the Roswell incident sparked widespread UFO sightings and conspiracy theories. The military's initial press release claimed recovery of a "flying disc," later retracted to a weather balloon. The memo in Ramey's hand became a focal point for those seeking evidence of a cover-up. The photo has been analyzed extensively, with some institutions offering rewards for a definitive reading of the telegram text.
