# Angela Ahrendts’ first email leading Apple retail

**Overview**
This memo was Angela Ahrendts’ first communication to Apple retail employees after joining Apple as Senior Vice President of Retail in 2014.

**Key Points**
- Emphasized enriching lives and communities through Apple Stores.
- Outlined her vision for integrating online and physical retail experiences.
- Called for teamwork and a customer-centric culture.

**Context**
Sent in June 2014, the memo arrived as Ahrendts began her role overseeing Apple’s global retail operations. It signaled her focus on purpose-driven retail and set expectations for employee engagement.

**Relevance**
As an internal memo, it provides insight into how a senior leader communicates strategy directly to employees without public relations filtering, aligning with the definition of a memo as raw, internal communication.
