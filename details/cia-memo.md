# CIA Memo

## Overview
The CIA Memo.jpg image shows excerpts from a Central Intelligence Agency memo dated August 2001, warning of a possible attack by Osama bin Laden on the United States.

## Description
The document is a scanned image of a declassified CIA memo, highlighting intelligence gathered prior to the September 11, 2001 attacks. It includes annotations and markings typical of intelligence briefings.

## Creation Process
The memo was created by CIA analysts in August 2001 as part of the President's Daily Brief (PDB) series. The image was later released by the National Security Archives and uploaded to Wikimedia Commons.

## Context
This memo is often cited in discussions about pre-9/11 intelligence failures and the Bush administration's awareness of al-Qaeda threats. It is referenced in the article "Bin Ladin Determined to Strike in US" (PDB of August 6, 2001).
