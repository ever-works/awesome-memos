# Winstin Churchill on brevity in government reports

## Overview
This memo, attributed to Winston Churchill, outlines his preference for concise government reporting. He argues that reports should focus on delivering main points through short, crisp paragraphs rather than lengthy exposition.

## Context
Written during Churchill's time in government, the memo reflects a broader push for efficiency and clarity in bureaucratic communication. Its advice remains relevant for modern professional writing.

## Significance
The memo is often cited in discussions about effective communication and the value of brevity in professional documents.
